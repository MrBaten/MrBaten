## 👋 About me

I'm a machine learning researcher from **Dhaka, Bangladesh**, working on **computer vision and AutoML**. I'm most interested in vision models that hold up on small, real-world data.

- 🔬 Co-authored **7 published papers** — shadow detection, crop leaf disease and insect identification, medical image classification, AutoML, and a mental-health dataset
- 🧪 Research Assistant at **Deep Lab** (May 2022 – Jan 2024)
- 🏢 Trainer, Digitization of Distributive Trade at **Unilever** (Sep 2025 – Jun 2026)
- 🎓 **B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering**, Northern University Bangladesh (2023)
- 🛠️ These days I build AI systems end to end — most recently **Simigo**, an AI executive assistant
- 📫 Reach me at **abubaten.a@gmail.com**
